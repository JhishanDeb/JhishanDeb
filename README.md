### Jhishan Deb

I'm a CS graduate of BRAC University (Dhaka) working on NLP and LLMs, mainly how
models use retrieved evidence and how to measure that reliably, often in biomedical settings.

**Research**
- [llm-evidence-reliance](https://github.com/JhishanDeb/llm-evidence-reliance): five pre-registered studies
  of whether open-weight LLMs follow a clinical risk model's SHAP explanation (1.3M+ prompts, 17 checkpoints).
  Manuscript in preparation for ACL 2027.
- Evidence-grounded LLMs for genetic variant interpretation: how open LLMs use literature evidence when
  classifying genetic variants. Manuscript in preparation for ACL 2027; code will be released with the paper.

**Projects**
- [medqa-confidence](https://github.com/JhishanDeb/medqa-confidence): pre-registered comparison of stated
  confidence, option probability and sample agreement for three open LLMs on all 1,273 MedQA test questions.
- [bangla-token-tax](https://github.com/JhishanDeb/bangla-token-tax): Bangla costs 2.7–5.8× more tokens
  than English in Gemma 2, Qwen 2.5 and Llama 3.1 (FLORES-200).
